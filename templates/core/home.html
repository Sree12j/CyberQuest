Try it now

Quick safety check

Choose a situation and reveal a practical tip. This mini-demo is local, unscored and fictional.

Scenario 01

“Your account closes in 10 minutes — confirm your password now.”

What is the safest first move?

Pause. Check the sender, avoid the link and open the service directly in your browser. Urgency is a common pressure tactic.

Scenario 02

You need a password for a new game account.

What would make it harder to guess?

Use a long, unique passphrase and a password manager. Add multi-factor authentication when the account offers it.

Scenario 03

A café lists two Wi-Fi names that look almost identical.

How do you choose?

Ask staff for the exact name. A network name is not proof of safety, so stick to HTTPS and avoid sensitive tasks on an untrusted connection.
